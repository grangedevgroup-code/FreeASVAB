# General Science

::: quiz gs3 | General Science – Practice Set 3 (Life Science) | group=bank | time=15 minutes
? Which part of a cell contains most of its genetic material?
- Cell membrane
- Nucleus *
- Cytoplasm
- Vacuole
> The **nucleus** holds the cell's DNA. The membrane controls what enters and leaves, and the cytoplasm is the fluid the organelles sit in.

? The organelle that releases energy from food for the cell is the:
- ribosome.
- chloroplast.
- mitochondrion. *
- Golgi body.
> **Mitochondria** carry out cellular respiration and release energy. Ribosomes build proteins; chloroplasts capture light in plants.

? Which blood cells help the body fight infection?
- Red blood cells
- Platelets
- Plasma cells only
- White blood cells *
> **White blood cells** attack germs. Red blood cells carry oxygen, and platelets help blood clot.

? Which vessels carry blood away from the heart?
- Arteries *
- Veins
- Capillaries
- Lymph ducts
> **Arteries** carry blood away from the heart. Veins return it, and capillaries connect the two.

? An organism that makes its own food from sunlight is called a:
- consumer.
- decomposer.
- producer. *
- predator.
> Plants and algae are **producers**: they make food by photosynthesis. Consumers eat other organisms.

? Which gas do plants take in from the air to make food?
- Oxygen
- Carbon dioxide *
- Nitrogen
- Hydrogen
> Plants use **carbon dioxide**, water and light energy to make glucose, and they release oxygen.

? The largest organ of the human body is the:
- liver.
- brain.
- skin. *
- small intestine.
> The **skin** is the largest organ by area and weight. The liver is the largest internal organ.

? Where does most digestion and absorption of food take place?
- Stomach
- Small intestine *
- Large intestine
- Esophagus
> Most nutrients are digested and absorbed in the **small intestine**. The large intestine mainly absorbs water.

? A vitamin that the body makes when skin is exposed to sunlight is vitamin:
- A.
- B~{12}.
- C.
- D. *
> Sunlight on the skin lets the body make **vitamin D**, which helps it absorb calcium.

? Lack of vitamin C can cause:
- scurvy. *
- rickets.
- night blindness.
- anemia.
> **Scurvy** (bleeding gums, slow healing) comes from too little vitamin C. Rickets comes from too little vitamin D.

? The part of the brain that controls balance and coordination is the:
- cerebrum.
- cerebellum. *
- medulla.
- hypothalamus.
> The **cerebellum** coordinates movement and balance. The cerebrum handles thinking; the medulla controls breathing and heart rate.

? Tissue that connects muscle to bone is called a:
- ligament.
- cartilage.
- tendon. *
- marrow.
> A **tendon** joins muscle to bone. A ligament joins bone to bone.

? In genetics, an organism with two different alleles for a trait (such as Bb) is:
- homozygous.
- heterozygous. *
- recessive.
- a mutation.
> **Heterozygous** means the two alleles differ. BB or bb would be homozygous.

? Which of these is a disease caused by a virus?
- Strep throat
- Tuberculosis
- Influenza *
- Tetanus
> **Influenza** (the flu) is viral. Strep throat, tuberculosis and tetanus are caused by bacteria, which is why antibiotics work on them but not on the flu.

? The process by which a cell divides to make two identical cells is:
- meiosis.
- mitosis. *
- fertilization.
- osmosis.
> **Mitosis** makes two identical body cells. Meiosis makes sex cells with half the chromosomes.

? Animals that keep a steady body temperature regardless of their surroundings are called:
- cold-blooded.
- warm-blooded. *
- amphibians.
- invertebrates.
> Birds and mammals are **warm-blooded** (endothermic). Reptiles and amphibians are cold-blooded.

? In a food chain, grass → grasshopper → frog → snake, the frog is a:
- producer.
- primary consumer.
- secondary consumer. *
- decomposer.
> The grasshopper eats the producer (primary consumer). The **frog eats the grasshopper**, so it is the secondary consumer.

? Which system carries messages through the body using electrical signals?
- Nervous system *
- Digestive system
- Skeletal system
- Excretory system
> The **nervous system** (brain, spinal cord and nerves) uses electrical signals. The endocrine system uses hormones instead.

? Insulin is made by the:
- liver.
- pancreas. *
- kidney.
- thyroid.
> The **pancreas** makes insulin, which lowers blood sugar.

? The scientific name *Homo sapiens* gives an organism's:
- kingdom and phylum.
- class and order.
- family and genus.
- genus and species. *
> Scientific names use two parts: the **genus** (*Homo*) and the **species** (*sapiens*).
:::

::: quiz gs4 | General Science – Practice Set 4 (Earth, Space, Chemistry, Physics) | group=bank | time=15 minutes
? The layer of the atmosphere where almost all weather happens is the:
- stratosphere.
- troposphere. *
- mesosphere.
- thermosphere.
> Weather happens in the **troposphere**, the lowest layer. The ozone layer is in the stratosphere.

? A scale used to measure the strength of earthquakes is the:
- Beaufort scale.
- Mohs scale.
- Richter scale. *
- Kelvin scale.
> The **Richter scale** (and the newer moment magnitude scale) measure earthquakes. Mohs measures mineral hardness; Beaufort measures wind.

? On the Mohs scale, the hardest natural mineral is:
- quartz.
- topaz.
- corundum.
- diamond. *
> **Diamond** is a 10, the top of the Mohs hardness scale.

? What causes the seasons on Earth?
- Earth's changing distance from the Sun
- The tilt of Earth's axis *
- The Moon's gravity
- Sunspots
> Seasons come from the **tilt of Earth's axis** (about 23.5°), which changes how directly sunlight hits each hemisphere through the year.

? The Moon's gravity is the main cause of:
- ocean tides. *
- earthquakes.
- trade winds.
- the seasons.
> The Moon's pull (with some help from the Sun) creates **tides**.

? Which planet is the largest in our solar system?
- Saturn
- Neptune
- Jupiter *
- Earth
> **Jupiter** is the largest planet, more than 11 times Earth's diameter.

? Light from the Sun takes about how long to reach Earth?
- 8 seconds
- 8 minutes *
- 8 hours
- 8 days
> Sunlight travels about 93 million miles in roughly **8 minutes**.

? A front where a cold air mass pushes under a warm air mass usually brings:
- clear, calm weather for days.
- thunderstorms and a temperature drop. *
- fog and drizzle only.
- a rise in temperature.
> A **cold front** forces warm air up quickly, often causing thunderstorms, then cooler air.

? Water boils at what temperature at sea level?
- 0 °C
- 32 °C
- 100 °C *
- 212 °C
> Water boils at **100 °C** (212 °F) at sea level. It freezes at 0 °C (32 °F).

? The center of an atom, which contains protons and neutrons, is the:
- electron cloud.
- nucleus. *
- shell.
- isotope.
> Protons and neutrons are packed in the **nucleus**. Electrons move around it.

? The atomic number of an element tells the number of:
- neutrons.
- protons. *
- molecules.
- isotopes.
> The **atomic number** is the number of protons, which decides what element it is.

? A solution with a pH of 3 is:
- strongly basic.
- neutral.
- acidic. *
- slightly basic.
> pH below 7 is **acidic**, 7 is neutral, above 7 is basic. A pH of 3 is quite acidic, like vinegar or soda.

? Rust forms when iron combines with:
- nitrogen.
- oxygen. *
- helium.
- carbon.
> Rusting is **oxidation**: iron reacts with oxygen (and water) to form iron oxide.

? Which is a chemical change?
- Ice melting
- Paper being cut
- Wood burning *
- Sugar dissolving in water
> **Burning** makes new substances (ash, gases). Melting, cutting and dissolving change form but not the substance.

? Which unit measures electrical power?
- Volt
- Ohm
- Ampere
- Watt *
> Power is measured in **watts**. Volts measure voltage, amperes measure current, ohms measure resistance.

? An object at rest stays at rest unless a force acts on it. This is Newton's:
- first law. *
- second law.
- third law.
- law of gravity.
> Newton's **first law** is the law of inertia.

? "For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction" is Newton's:
- first law.
- second law.
- third law. *
- law of motion and energy.
> That is Newton's **third law**. It explains how rockets push themselves forward.

? Heat moving through a metal spoon from the hot end to your hand is an example of:
- radiation.
- convection.
- conduction. *
- insulation.
> **Conduction** is heat moving through direct contact. Convection moves heat through fluids; radiation moves it as waves, like sunlight.

? Which type of electromagnetic wave has the shortest wavelength?
- Radio waves
- Visible light
- Microwaves
- Gamma rays *
> **Gamma rays** have the shortest wavelength and the most energy. Radio waves have the longest.

? Sound travels fastest through:
- a vacuum.
- air.
- water.
- steel. *
> Sound travels fastest through **solids** like steel, slower through water and slowest through air. It cannot travel through a vacuum at all.
