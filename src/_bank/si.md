# Shop Information

::: quiz si2 | Shop Information – Practice Set 2 | group=bank | time=12 minutes
? Which tool is best for tightening a hex nut in a tight space where a wrench cannot swing far?
- Pipe wrench
- Ratchet and socket *
- Claw hammer
- Vise grip
> A **ratchet** turns the socket in short swings without lifting it off the nut.

? A torque wrench is used to:
- loosen rusted bolts.
- tighten a fastener to an exact specification. *
- cut threads in a hole.
- measure the length of a bolt.
> A **torque wrench** shows or limits how hard a bolt is tightened, so parts are not under- or over-tightened.

? Which saw is designed to cut metal?
- Crosscut saw
- Coping saw
- Hacksaw *
- Rip saw
> A **hacksaw** has fine teeth and a hardened blade for cutting metal.

? A rip saw is used to cut wood:
- across the grain.
- along (with) the grain. *
- in curves.
- into thin veneers.
> A **rip saw** cuts with the grain; a **crosscut saw** cuts across it.

? A coping saw is best for:
- cutting thick logs.
- cutting curves and detailed shapes in thin wood. *
- cutting metal pipe.
- cutting along long, straight boards.
> The **coping saw**'s thin blade turns easily to follow curves.

? Which tool is used to cut internal threads in a drilled hole?
- Die
- Tap *
- Reamer
- Countersink
> A **tap** cuts threads inside a hole. A **die** cuts threads on the outside of a rod.

? A die is used to:
- cut external threads on a rod or bolt. *
- smooth the inside of a hole.
- drive nails below the surface.
- measure thread pitch.
> A **die** cuts the outside threads that let a rod accept a nut.

? A nail set is used to:
- pull out bent nails.
- drive the head of a finish nail below the surface. *
- measure nail length.
- hold nails while you start them.
> A **nail set** sinks the nail head so the hole can be filled and hidden.

? Which hammer is best for shaping metal and setting rivets?
- Claw hammer
- Ball-peen hammer *
- Rubber mallet
- Tack hammer
> A **ball-peen hammer** has a rounded peen for shaping metal and setting rivets.

? A rubber or wooden mallet is used when you want to:
- drive large spikes.
- strike without damaging the surface. *
- break concrete.
- set rivets.
> A **mallet** delivers a soft blow that will not dent or mar the work, such as when tapping a chisel or joining wood.

? A level is used to check whether a surface is:
- perfectly horizontal or vertical. *
- smooth.
- square at the corners.
- the correct length.
> A **level** uses a bubble in liquid to show true horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb).

? A plumb bob shows:
- true vertical. *
- true horizontal.
- the depth of a hole.
- the angle of a roof.
> A weighted **plumb bob** hangs straight down, marking true vertical.

? Which tool measures the inside or outside diameter of an object most precisely?
- Tape measure
- Yardstick
- Micrometer *
- Carpenter's square
> A **micrometer** measures to thousandths of an inch or better. Calipers are also used, but the micrometer is the most precise here.

? A carpenter's square (framing square) is mainly used to:
- check and mark 90-degree angles. *
- measure curves.
- cut metal.
- level shelves.
> A **framing square** lays out and checks right angles.

? A chisel should be driven with:
- a claw hammer on a steel chisel only.
- a mallet for wood chisels. *
- a pipe wrench.
- a screwdriver handle.
> Wood chisels are struck with a **mallet** so the handle is not split.

? Which fastener is designed to be installed with a nut and goes all the way through the materials?
- Wood screw
- Bolt *
- Lag screw
- Finishing nail
> A **bolt** passes through the parts and is held by a nut.

? A lock washer is used to:
- seal out water.
- keep a nut from loosening from vibration. *
- spread out the load on soft wood.
- make a bolt longer.
> A **lock washer** bites into the nut and surface to keep the nut from working loose.

? A flat washer is mainly used to:
- spread the load of a bolt head or nut over a larger area. *
- lock the nut in place.
- make threads.
- conduct electricity.
> A **flat washer** spreads the clamping force and protects the surface.

? Which screwdriver tip is shaped like a cross?
- Slotted (flat)
- Phillips *
- Torx
- Hex
> A **Phillips** tip is cross-shaped. Torx is a six-point star; hex is a six-sided recess.

? Which pliers are best for gripping small objects in tight spaces?
- Slip-joint pliers
- Needle-nose pliers *
- Lineman's pliers
- Water-pump pliers
> **Needle-nose pliers** have long, thin jaws for small parts and tight spots.

? Locking pliers (vise grips) are best for:
- cutting wire.
- clamping an object firmly so your hands are free. *
- bending sheet metal into curves.
- measuring wire gauge.
> **Locking pliers** clamp and stay locked on the work.

? A countersink bit is used to:
- drill a cone-shaped recess so a flat-head screw sits flush. *
- enlarge a hole to an exact size.
- cut threads.
- drill through glass.
> A **countersink** makes a cone-shaped seat so the screw head lies flush with the surface.

? A plane is used on wood to:
- cut joints.
- shave and smooth the surface. *
- drill holes.
- measure thickness.
> A **plane** shaves thin layers to smooth or flatten wood.

? Which file has the finest cut?
- Bastard file
- Second-cut file
- Smooth file *
- Rasp
> From coarse to fine: rasp, bastard, second-cut, **smooth**.

? Which is the safest practice when using a grinder?
- Remove the guard for better visibility.
- Wear safety glasses or a face shield. *
- Grind on the side of the wheel.
- Wear loose gloves near the wheel.
> Always **protect your eyes**. Keep guards on, use the face of the wheel, and keep loose clothing away.

? Plywood is made by:
- pressing sawdust and glue together.
- gluing thin layers of wood with the grain of each layer turned crosswise. *
- cutting a single board from a log.
- laminating plastic over pine.
> **Plywood**'s crossed layers make it strong and resistant to splitting. Pressed sawdust is particleboard.

? Which wood is a hardwood?
- Pine
- Fir
- Oak *
- Cedar
> **Oak** comes from a broad-leaved tree and is a hardwood. Pine, fir and cedar are softwoods.

? In welding, the main purpose of flux is to:
- cool the weld quickly.
- protect the molten metal from contamination by air. *
- add color to the weld.
- make the metal softer.
> **Flux** shields the weld from oxygen and other contaminants.

? A nail's size marked "16d" refers to its:
- diameter in sixteenths.
- penny size, which indicates its length. *
- weight in pounds.
- number per box.
> The "d" stands for **penny**; a 16d nail is about 3½ inches long.

? Which tool would you use to cut a large round hole in a door for a lockset?
- Twist bit
- Hole saw *
- Spade file
- Countersink
> A **hole saw** cuts large circular holes, such as for door hardware.
