# Electronics Information

::: quiz ei3 | Electronics Information – Practice Set 3 | group=bank | time=12 minutes
? A circuit has a 12-volt battery and a 4-ohm resistor. What is the current?
- 0.33 amperes
- 3 amperes *
- 16 amperes
- 48 amperes
> Ohm's law: I = V ÷ R = 12 ÷ 4 = **3 A**.
= 12/4

? A current of 2 amperes flows through a 10-ohm resistor. What is the voltage across it?
- 5 volts
- 8 volts
- 12 volts
- 20 volts *
> V = I × R = 2 × 10 = **20 V**.
= 2*10

? Three 6-ohm resistors are connected in series. What is the total resistance?
- 2 ohms
- 6 ohms
- 18 ohms *
- 36 ohms
> In series, resistances add: 6 + 6 + 6 = **18 Ω**.
= 6+6+6

? Two 10-ohm resistors are connected in parallel. What is the total resistance?
- 5 ohms *
- 10 ohms
- 20 ohms
- 100 ohms
> Two equal resistors in parallel give half of one: 10 ÷ 2 = **5 Ω**.
= 10*10/(10+10)

? A 120-volt appliance draws 5 amperes. How much power does it use?
- 24 watts
- 115 watts
- 600 watts *
- 1,200 watts
> P = V × I = 120 × 5 = **600 W**.
= 120*5

? Which material is the best conductor of electricity?
- Rubber
- Glass
- Copper *
- Dry wood
> **Copper** is an excellent conductor, which is why most wiring uses it. The others are insulators.

? A device that stores electrical charge is a:
- resistor.
- capacitor. *
- diode.
- fuse.
> A **capacitor** stores charge between two plates separated by an insulator.

? A diode allows current to flow:
- in one direction only. *
- in both directions equally.
- only when it is cold.
- only in AC circuits.
> A **diode** acts like a one-way valve for current. It is used to turn AC into DC.

? The unit of resistance is the:
- volt.
- ohm. *
- farad.
- henry.
> Resistance is measured in **ohms (Ω)**. Farads measure capacitance; henrys measure inductance.

? Household current in the United States is:
- 60 Hz alternating current. *
- 50 Hz direct current.
- 12-volt direct current.
- 220 Hz alternating current.
> U.S. homes use **alternating current at 60 hertz**, about 120 volts at standard outlets.

? A transformer is used to:
- change DC into AC.
- store energy for later.
- raise or lower AC voltage. *
- measure current.
> A **transformer** steps AC voltage up or down. It does not work on steady DC.

? A transformer has 100 turns on its primary coil and 500 turns on its secondary. If 120 volts goes in, about how much comes out?
- 24 volts
- 120 volts
- 480 volts
- 600 volts *
> The voltage changes by the turns ratio: 120 × (500 ÷ 100) = **600 V**. This is a step-up transformer.
= 120*500/100

? A fuse protects a circuit by:
- raising the voltage.
- melting and opening the circuit when current is too high. *
- storing extra current.
- grounding the circuit permanently.
> A **fuse** contains a thin strip that melts when too much current flows, breaking the circuit.

? In a series circuit, if one bulb burns out:
- the other bulbs get brighter.
- only that bulb goes dark.
- all the bulbs go out. *
- the battery drains faster.
> A series circuit has only one path. A burned-out bulb **opens the path**, so everything stops.

? In a parallel circuit, the voltage across each branch is:
- the same as the source voltage. *
- divided equally among branches.
- zero.
- higher than the source voltage.
> Each branch of a parallel circuit gets the **full source voltage**. The current divides among the branches.

? The color band code on a resistor shows its:
- power rating only.
- resistance value. *
- voltage limit.
- operating temperature.
> The colored bands give the **resistance value** and tolerance.

? Which instrument measures electrical current?
- Voltmeter
- Ammeter *
- Ohmmeter
- Wattmeter
> An **ammeter** measures current and is connected in series. A voltmeter measures voltage and connects in parallel.

? A semiconductor device that can amplify signals or act as a switch is a:
- transistor. *
- resistor.
- fuse.
- transformer.
> The **transistor** amplifies or switches signals and is the building block of modern electronics.

? Which wire color is normally used for the ground wire in U.S. household wiring?
- Black
- Red
- White
- Green or bare copper *
> **Green or bare copper** is ground. Black and red are usually hot; white is usually neutral.

? What is the purpose of the third (round) prong on a plug?
- It carries extra current.
- It grounds the appliance for safety. *
- It raises the voltage.
- It turns the appliance off.
> The round prong connects the appliance case to **ground**, so a fault sends current safely away instead of through you.

? An inductor resists changes in:
- voltage.
- current. *
- resistance.
- frequency only.
> An **inductor** (a coil) stores energy in a magnetic field and opposes changes in current.

? One kilowatt equals:
- 10 watts.
- 100 watts.
- 1,000 watts. *
- 1,000,000 watts.
> *Kilo* means a thousand: 1 kW = **1,000 W**.

? A 60-watt bulb runs for 10 hours. How many kilowatt-hours does it use?
- 0.06
- 0.6 *
- 6
- 600
> 60 W × 10 h = 600 watt-hours = **0.6 kWh**.
= 60*10/1000

? Which of these produces direct current (DC)?
- A wall outlet
- A battery *
- A power-company generator
- A step-down transformer
> A **battery** produces DC. Wall outlets and utility generators supply AC.

? The symbol on a schematic that looks like a zigzag line stands for a:
- capacitor.
- resistor. *
- battery.
- switch.
> A **zigzag** is the standard resistor symbol. Two parallel lines are a capacitor; long and short lines are a battery.
:::
