# Mechanical Comprehension

::: quiz mc3 | Mechanical Comprehension – Practice Set 3 | group=bank | time=22 minutes
@instr: Choose the best answer. Ignore friction and the weight of levers, ropes, and pulleys unless the question says otherwise.
? A 60-pound weight sits 2 feet from the fulcrum of a lever. How much force is needed 6 feet from the fulcrum on the other side to balance it?
- 10 pounds
- 20 pounds *
- 30 pounds
- 180 pounds
> Weight × distance on each side must match: 60 × 2 = F × 6, so F = 120 ÷ 6 = **20 lb**.
= 60*2/6

? A lever has an effort arm of 8 feet and a load arm of 2 feet. What is its mechanical advantage?
- 2
- 4 *
- 6
- 16
> Mechanical advantage = effort arm ÷ load arm = 8 ÷ 2 = **4**.
= 8/2

? A single fixed pulley is used to lift a 50-pound load. What force is needed?
- 25 pounds
- 50 pounds *
- 100 pounds
- 12.5 pounds
> A single fixed pulley only **changes the direction** of the force. Its mechanical advantage is 1, so you still pull **50 lb**.
= 50

? A load is supported by 4 strands of rope in a block-and-tackle. How much force lifts a 200-pound load?
- 25 pounds
- 50 pounds *
- 100 pounds
- 200 pounds
> The mechanical advantage equals the number of supporting strands: 200 ÷ 4 = **50 lb**.
= 200/4

? A ramp is 12 feet long and rises 3 feet. About how much force is needed to push a 120-pound box up it?
- 10 pounds
- 30 pounds *
- 40 pounds
- 120 pounds
> MA = length ÷ height = 12 ÷ 3 = 4. Force = 120 ÷ 4 = **30 lb**.
= 120*3/12

? Gear A has 20 teeth and drives gear B, which has 40 teeth. If gear A turns 100 times, how many times does gear B turn?
- 25
- 50 *
- 100
- 200
> Turns × teeth stay equal: 100 × 20 = B × 40, so B = **50 turns**. The larger gear turns more slowly.
= 100*20/40

? Two meshed gears always turn:
- in the same direction.
- in opposite directions. *
- at the same speed.
- only when an idler gear is added.
> Meshed gears turn in **opposite directions**. An idler gear between them makes the first and last turn the same way.

? Three gears are meshed in a row. If the first gear turns clockwise, the third gear turns:
- clockwise. *
- counterclockwise.
- not at all.
- back and forth.
> Each mesh reverses direction: clockwise → counterclockwise → **clockwise**.

? A small pulley drives a larger pulley with a belt. Compared with the small pulley, the large pulley turns:
- faster.
- slower. *
- at the same speed.
- in the opposite direction.
> The belt moves the same distance on both, so the **larger pulley turns more slowly** (but with more torque).

? When a belt between two pulleys is crossed (twisted into a figure 8), the pulleys turn:
- in the same direction.
- in opposite directions. *
- at the same speed always.
- not at all.
> A **crossed belt** reverses the direction of the driven pulley.

? A wheel-and-axle has a wheel radius of 15 inches and an axle radius of 3 inches. What is its mechanical advantage?
- 3
- 5 *
- 12
- 45
> MA = wheel radius ÷ axle radius = 15 ÷ 3 = **5**.
= 15/3

? A screw jack's mechanical advantage increases when the threads are:
- farther apart.
- closer together. *
- painted.
- made of plastic.
> **Closer threads** (smaller pitch) mean more turns per inch of lift, so less force is needed.

? In a hydraulic system, a 10-pound force on a 1-square-inch piston pushes on a 20-square-inch piston. What force does the large piston exert?
- 10 pounds
- 20 pounds
- 100 pounds
- 200 pounds *
> Pressure is the same everywhere: 10 lb per square inch × 20 square inches = **200 lb**.
= 10*20

? Water flows through a pipe that narrows. In the narrow section, the water flows:
- slower.
- faster. *
- at the same speed.
- backward.
> The same amount of water must pass every point, so it **speeds up** where the pipe is narrower.

? Which shape is the strongest for a beam that will carry a load from above?
- A flat board laid on its wide face
- The same board standing on its narrow edge *
- A round dowel of the same weight
- A thin sheet of metal
> A board **on edge** is much stiffer because more of its material is far from the center of bending.

? An object will be most stable (least likely to tip over) if it has:
- a high center of gravity and a narrow base.
- a low center of gravity and a wide base. *
- a high center of gravity and a wide base.
- a low center of gravity and a narrow base.
> A **low center of gravity and a wide base** keep the object from tipping.

? A 150-pound person stands on a board resting on two supports, exactly halfway between them. How much weight does each support carry?
- 50 pounds
- 75 pounds *
- 150 pounds
- 300 pounds
> At the midpoint the load is shared equally: 150 ÷ 2 = **75 lb** each.
= 150/2

? A force of 50 pounds moves a box 10 feet. How much work is done?
- 5 foot-pounds
- 60 foot-pounds
- 500 foot-pounds *
- 5,000 foot-pounds
> Work = force × distance = 50 × 10 = **500 ft-lb**.
= 50*10

? Which simple machine is a knife blade an example of?
- Lever
- Wedge *
- Pulley
- Wheel and axle
> A blade is a **wedge**: it changes a downward push into sideways force that splits material.

? A spring stretches 2 inches when 10 pounds hangs from it. How far will it stretch with 25 pounds?
- 2.5 inches
- 4 inches
- 5 inches *
- 12.5 inches
> Spring stretch is proportional to force: 10 lb per 2 inches is 5 lb per inch. 25 ÷ 5 = **5 inches**.
= 25*2/10

? Which of these uses the principle of a first-class lever (fulcrum between effort and load)?
- Wheelbarrow
- Seesaw *
- Fishing rod
- Nutcracker
> A **seesaw** has the fulcrum in the middle. A wheelbarrow and nutcracker are second class; a fishing rod is third class.

? A wheelbarrow is a second-class lever because:
- the effort is between the fulcrum and the load.
- the load is between the fulcrum and the effort. *
- the fulcrum is between the load and the effort.
- it has no fulcrum.
> The wheel is the fulcrum, the load sits in the middle, and you lift at the handles: **load in the middle**.

? Ice skates glide easily because:
- ice has high friction.
- a thin layer of water reduces friction. *
- the blades are heavy.
- skates use ball bearings.
> A thin film of water on the ice surface **reduces friction** under the blade.

? Ball bearings are used in machines mainly to:
- add weight.
- reduce friction. *
- increase speed by themselves.
- store energy.
> Rolling **ball bearings** replace sliding friction with much lower rolling friction.

? A cam converts:
- straight motion into rotary motion only.
- rotary motion into up-and-down (reciprocating) motion. *
- heat into motion.
- electricity into motion.
> A rotating **cam** pushes a follower up and down, as it does to open engine valves.
