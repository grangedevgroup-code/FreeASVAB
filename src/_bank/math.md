# Math Practice

::: quiz ar4 | Arithmetic Reasoning – Practice Set 4 | group=bank | time=30 minutes
@instr: Solve each problem without a calculator.
? A recruit runs 3 miles on Monday, 4.5 miles on Wednesday, and 5 miles on Friday. How many miles does the recruit run in all?
- 11.5
- 12
- 12.5 *
- 13
> 3 + 4.5 + 5 = **12.5 miles**.
= 3+4.5+5

? A box holds 24 rations. How many boxes are needed for 150 rations?
- 6
- 6.25
- 7 *
- 8
> 150 ÷ 24 = 6.25. You cannot order part of a box, so you need **7 boxes**.
= 7

? A truck uses 1 gallon of fuel every 8 miles. How many gallons does it need for a 260-mile trip?
- 30
- 32.5 *
- 35
- 40
> 260 ÷ 8 = **32.5 gallons**.
= 260/8

? A uniform costs $80. The price goes up 15%. What is the new price?
- $82
- $90
- $92 *
- $95
> 15% of $80 is $12. $80 + $12 = **$92**.
= 80*1.15

? A soldier's paycheck is $2,400. If 22% is withheld for taxes, how much is withheld?
- $480
- $528 *
- $540
- $1,872
> 0.22 × 2,400 = **$528**.
= 0.22*2400

? A map scale shows 1 inch = 25 miles. Two bases are 3.5 inches apart on the map. How far apart are they?
- 75 miles
- 80 miles
- 87.5 miles *
- 92.5 miles
> 3.5 × 25 = **87.5 miles**.
= 3.5*25

? The ratio of trucks to jeeps in a motor pool is 3 to 5. If there are 15 trucks, how many jeeps are there?
- 9
- 20
- 25 *
- 30
> 15 is 3 × 5, so multiply the jeep part by 5 too: 5 × 5 = **25**.
= 15*5/3

? A plane flies 1,800 miles in 4 hours. What is its average speed?
- 400 mph
- 450 mph *
- 500 mph
- 7,200 mph
> 1,800 ÷ 4 = **450 miles per hour**.
= 1800/4

? Two trains leave the same station at the same time going in opposite directions. One travels 50 mph and the other 70 mph. How far apart are they after 3 hours?
- 60 miles
- 210 miles
- 360 miles *
- 420 miles
> Moving apart, their speeds add: 50 + 70 = 120 mph. 120 × 3 = **360 miles**.
= (50+70)*3

? A tank is 3/4 full and holds 60 gallons when full. How many gallons are in it?
- 15
- 40
- 45 *
- 48
> 3/4 × 60 = **45 gallons**.
= 60*3/4

? A squad of 9 people shares 2 pizzas cut into 12 slices each. If everyone eats the same number of whole slices, how many slices are left over?
- 0
- 3
- 6 *
- 9
> 24 slices ÷ 9 people = 2 slices each with 24 − 18 = **6** left over.
= 24-18

? A store sells 3 notebooks for $4.50. How much do 7 notebooks cost?
- $9.00
- $10.50 *
- $12.00
- $31.50
> One notebook costs $4.50 ÷ 3 = $1.50, and 7 × $1.50 = **$10.50**.
= 7*4.5/3

? An account earns 5% simple interest per year. How much interest does $1,200 earn in 3 years?
- $60
- $150
- $180 *
- $360
> 1,200 × 0.05 × 3 = **$180**.
= 1200*0.05*3

? The temperature was −8°F at dawn and rose 21 degrees by noon. What was the temperature at noon?
- −13°F
- 13°F *
- 21°F
- 29°F
> −8 + 21 = **13°F**.
= -8+21

? A recipe for 4 people uses 6 cups of rice. How many cups are needed for 10 people?
- 12
- 15 *
- 16
- 24
> 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5 cups per person, and 1.5 × 10 = **15 cups**.
= 6*10/4

? A soldier scored 82, 90, and 77 on three tests. What is the average?
- 81
- 83 *
- 84
- 85
> (82 + 90 + 77) ÷ 3 = 249 ÷ 3 = **83**.
= (82+90+77)/3

? A rope 45 feet long is cut into pieces 2.5 feet long. How many pieces are there?
- 15
- 18 *
- 20
- 22
> 45 ÷ 2.5 = **18 pieces**.
= 45/2.5

? Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours. Pipe B fills it in 3 hours. How long do they take together?
- 1.5 hours
- 2 hours *
- 4.5 hours
- 9 hours
> Together they fill 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2 of the tank per hour, so the job takes **2 hours**.
= 6*3/(6+3)

? A $250 radio is on sale for 20% off, and then a 6% sales tax is added to the sale price. What is the final cost?
- $200.00
- $212.00 *
- $215.00
- $265.00
> 20% off $250 is $200. Tax is 6% of $200 = $12. Total **$212**.
= 250*0.8*1.06

? It is 1430 (2:30 p.m.) now. What time will it be in 9 hours and 45 minutes, in 24-hour time?
- 2315
- 0015 *
- 0045
- 2415
> 1430 + 9 hours = 2330. Plus 45 minutes = 0015 the next day, so the answer is **0015**.

? Solve for *x*: 5*x* − 7 = 18
- 2.2
- 5 *
- 11
- 25
> Add 7: 5*x* = 25. Divide by 5: *x* = **5**.
= 25/5

? If 3(*x* + 4) = 27, what is *x*?
- 5 *
- 7
- 9
- 11
> Divide by 3: *x* + 4 = 9, so *x* = **5**.
= 27/3-4

? What is 2^{5}?
- 10
- 25
- 32 *
- 64
> 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = **32**.
= 2**5

? Simplify: (−4) × (−6)
- −24
- −10
- 10
- 24 *
> A negative times a negative is positive: **24**.
= (-4)*(-6)

? What is the value of 3*a* + 2*b* when *a* = 4 and *b* = −5?
- −2
- 2 *
- 12
- 22
> 3(4) + 2(−5) = 12 − 10 = **2**.
= 3*4+2*(-5)

? Which is the reciprocal of 2/7?
- −2/7
- 7/2 *
- 0.27
- 14
> The reciprocal flips the fraction: [[7/2]].

? What is the least common multiple of 6 and 8?
- 12
- 24 *
- 36
- 48
> Multiples of 8: 8, 16, **24**. 24 is the first that 6 also divides.
= lcm(6,8)

? Solve the inequality: 2*x* + 3 > 11
- *x* > 4 *
- *x* > 7
- *x* < 4
- *x* > 5.5
> Subtract 3: 2*x* > 8. Divide by 2: ***x* > 4**.

? What is 0.6 written as a fraction in lowest terms?
- 6/100
- 3/5 *
- 2/3
- 6/9
> 0.6 = 6/10 = **[[3/5]]**.

? The perimeter of a square is 36 inches. What is its area?
- 36 square inches
- 72 square inches
- 81 square inches *
- 144 square inches
> Each side is 36 ÷ 4 = 9 inches, so the area is 9 × 9 = **81 square inches**.
= (36/4)**2

? What is the volume of a rectangular box 5 feet long, 4 feet wide, and 3 feet high?
- 12 cubic feet
- 47 cubic feet
- 60 cubic feet *
- 94 cubic feet
> Volume = length × width × height = 5 × 4 × 3 = **60 cubic feet**.
= 5*4*3

? A circle has a diameter of 10 inches. What is its area? (Use π ≈ 3.14.)
- 31.4 square inches
- 78.5 square inches *
- 157 square inches
- 314 square inches
> The radius is 5. Area = π*r*^2 = 3.14 × 25 = **78.5 square inches**.
= 3.14*25

? Two angles are supplementary. One measures 65°. What does the other measure?
- 25°
- 115° *
- 125°
- 295°
> Supplementary angles add to 180°: 180 − 65 = **115°**.
= 180-65

? Two angles are complementary. One measures 38°. What does the other measure?
- 52° *
- 62°
- 142°
- 322°
> Complementary angles add to 90°: 90 − 38 = **52°**.
= 90-38

? How many degrees are in the sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral?
- 180
- 270
- 360 *
- 540
> Any quadrilateral splits into two triangles: 2 × 180 = **360°**.
= 2*180

? Factor: *x*^2 − 9
- (*x* − 3)^2
- (*x* + 3)(*x* − 3) *
- (*x* − 9)(*x* + 1)
- *x*(*x* − 9)
> This is a difference of squares: *a*^2 − *b*^2 = (*a* + *b*)(*a* − *b*), so the answer is **(*x* + 3)(*x* − 3)**.

? Solve: *x*^2 = 49
- *x* = 7 only
- *x* = 7 or *x* = −7 *
- *x* = 24.5
- *x* = 49 or *x* = −49
> Both 7 × 7 and (−7) × (−7) equal 49, so ***x* = 7 or −7**.

? What is the slope of the line *y* = −3*x* + 5?
- −3 *
- 3
- 5
- −5
> In *y* = *mx* + *b*, the slope is *m*: **−3**.

? Write 4.2 × 10^{3} in standard form.
- 42
- 420
- 4,200 *
- 42,000
> Move the decimal 3 places right: **4,200**.
= 4.2*10**3

? Which number is a prime number?
- 21
- 27
- 29 *
- 33
> **29** has no factors other than 1 and itself. 21 = 3 × 7, 27 = 3 × 9 and 33 = 3 × 11.
