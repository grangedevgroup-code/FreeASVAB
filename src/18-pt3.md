# Practice Test 3: AFQT Booster {.nonum data-label="AFQT Practice Test" data-sub="105 questions on the four subtests that make up your AFQT, with the paper-test lengths and time limits"}

## How to Take This Test

This test gives you extra practice on the four subtests that decide your AFQT: **Arithmetic Reasoning, Word Knowledge, Paragraph Comprehension,** and **Mathematics Knowledge**. It uses the number of questions and the time limits of the **paper** ASVAB, which is faster-paced than the computer test — good training for focus and speed. Many questions are harder than average, so a strong score here is a sign you are ready to push past a 70.

| Part | Subtest | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arithmetic Reasoning | 30 | 36 min |
| 2 | Word Knowledge | 35 | 11 min |
| 3 | Paragraph Comprehension | 15 | 13 min |
| 4 | Mathematics Knowledge | 25 | 24 min |
{.compact}

On this test you **may** skip a hard question and come back to it within the same part, as you could on the paper ASVAB. Answer every question before time runs out — a blank is always wrong.
